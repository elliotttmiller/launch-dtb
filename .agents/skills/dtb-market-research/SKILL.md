# DTB Market Research

Use current retrieval for changing claims. Separate modes when useful: landscape, competitor deep-dive, pricing/spec benchmark, news pulse, whitespace, supply/regulatory risk, contractor sentiment.

Match evidence to claim type, date volatile prices, corroborate important claims and label community evidence as sentiment rather than statistics. Distinguish sourced data from inference/hypothesis. Do not fabricate private DTB performance data.
