# DTB Market Research

## Scope first
Define question, market/geography, product segment, timeframe, comparison basis, and decision the research must support. Separate modes when useful: landscape, competitor deep dive, product/spec benchmark, pricing, demand signal, news pulse, whitespace, supply/regulatory risk, or contractor sentiment.

## Evidence hierarchy
Use manufacturer/provider/regulator sources for authoritative facts; reputable retailer/distributor sources for observed current pricing/availability; credible trade/statistical sources for broader market claims; and community sources for sentiment/anecdote only. Search snippets are discovery aids, not sufficient evidence for consequential claims.

Record retrieval dates for volatile facts. Normalize units, currency, pack size, configuration, tax/shipping assumptions and product generation before comparing numbers. Clearly distinguish MSRP/list, sale price, marketplace price, advertised price and out-of-stock observations.

Corroborate important or contested claims where feasible and note contradictions rather than averaging incompatible evidence. Separate sourced fact, calculation, inference, hypothesis and recommendation. Never fabricate private DTB sales, demand, margin or conversion metrics.

## Output quality
Lead with decision-relevant conclusions, then show evidence and limitations. Include comparable tables/calculations when they improve understanding, source provenance and dates, confidence, missing data, and concrete implications. Do not turn weak proxies (search count, review count, social mentions) into claimed sales volume without a defensible methodology.
