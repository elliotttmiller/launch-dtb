# Drywall Toolbox design system

Drywall Toolbox is a direct, professional ecommerce experience for drywall contractors. The visual language is dark navy, precise, restrained, and product-led rather than decorative.

## Core direction

- Use `#070d1c` as the hero base and `#2255ee` as the primary action/accent.
- Use white and cool slate copy with strong contrast and restrained typography.
- Prefer Geist for display text and Nunito/system sans for UI text.
- Use subtle depth, 1px translucent borders, and small atmospheric blue light fields.
- Motion should be transform/opacity based, slow, low-amplitude, and disabled or simplified under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Preserve clear product/navigation hierarchy and contractor-focused clarity.

## Avoid

- Generic SaaS/dashboard styling, excessive glass cards, particles, video backgrounds, large blur filters, novelty gradients, fake trust marks, and motion that competes with shopping.
